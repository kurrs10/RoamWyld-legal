Built for real multi-country trips

Most apps assume one destination. Roam Wyld assumes five. Entry requirements, emergency info, and language phrases adapt per country — automatically, based on your itinerary.

Actually works offline

Not "works offline if you remembered to open it before flying." Every bit of your trip data is cached locally before you leave. Airplane mode, no signal, cruise ship WiFi — you're covered.

Passport-aware, not generic

Entry requirements differ depending on where you're from. Roam Wyld filters visa rules, validity requirements, and entry checklists to your actual passport nationality — not a generic country page.

No spreadsheet required

"Adventure is out there" — and it shouldn't require a 14-tab spreadsheet to manage. Connect Gmail and Roam Wyld pulls your confirmation emails automatically. You review, tap to add. Done.
