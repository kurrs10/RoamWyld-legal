Delete Your Roam Wyld Account

How to permanently delete your Roam Wyld account and the data tied to it.

Option 1: Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Roam Wyld and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted right away. This works the same on iPhone and Android.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, or can’t sign in:

  1. Email support@roamwyld.app with the subject “Delete my Roam Wyld account.”
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Roam Wyld, so we can confirm the account is yours.

We’ll confirm by reply and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in email
  • All trips and bookings you created, including confirmation numbers
  • Your travel profile (passport nationality, home currency)
  • Travel insurance details you added
  • Trip invitations you sent, and your access to trips shared with you

What may be kept

  • Anonymous usage analytics that can’t be linked back to you, kept as part of aggregate statistics.
  • Crash and error logs, which contain no trip data, deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • An anonymous deletion record (date, account age in days, and number of trips), with no email or other identifying information.

If you connected Gmail, you can also revoke Roam Wyld’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

For more detail, see our Privacy Policy.
