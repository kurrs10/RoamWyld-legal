Release Notes

Every update to Roam Wyld — what changed and why.

Version 1.3 August 2026 Improvement

Trip Sharing

  • Trip owners now show their real name to invited travel partners.
  • Choose a default sharing setting for new bookings when you invite a travel partner.
  • Fixed an issue where a travel partner joining a trip could see an incorrect "joined your trip" notification.

Flights

  • Flight duration, layover time, and departure/arrival times now display automatically for Gmail-imported and manually-entered flights.
  • When traveling with a partner on the same flight, both seat numbers are shown together.

Import

  • Photo import now supports selecting multiple photos at once instead of one at a time.
  • Added a warning when an imported booking falls outside your trip's dates.
  • Added duplicate-booking detection when importing or adding manually.

Discover

  • Choose the date and time when adding a Discover suggestion to your trip.
  • Fixed an issue where adding a suggestion from Discover could silently fail to add it to your itinerary.

General

  • Entry requirements now display correctly for every destination and passport nationality.
  • Today now correctly displays scheduled flights and bookings for the current day.
  • Performance improvements and bug fixes.
Version 1.2 July 9, 2026 Improvement

Trip Sharing

  • Shared trips — invite a travel partner to see your full itinerary on their app. They'll see all your flights, hotels, and activities in real time.

Gmail Import

  • Gmail import now automatically detects Airbnb experiences, tours, and activities alongside flights and hotels.

General

  • Performance improvements and bug fixes.
Version 1.1 July 2026 Improvement

Gmail Import

  • Gmail import now searches all folders and custom labels — emails filed away from your inbox are found.
  • Added 50+ international airlines and booking platforms including Singapore Air, Qantas, Emirates, Garuda, Korean Air, AirAsia, LATAM, Avianca, and more.
  • Expanded booking keywords so more confirmation email formats are recognized.
  • Full email body now captured for more emails before sending to parsing — reduces missing details like confirmation numbers and dates.
  • Added car rental as a distinct booking type (Hertz, Enterprise, Sixt, Europcar, etc.).

General

  • Performance improvements and bug fixes.
Version 1.0 July 6, 2026 Launch

Trip Management

  • Create trips with multiple destinations, travel dates, and passport nationality.
  • Add flights, hotels, and activities manually or via Gmail import.
  • Flight numbers validated against live airline data before saving.
  • Day-by-day itinerary view with all bookings in one timeline.

Entry Requirements

  • Visa rules, passport validity, and entry checklists for 50+ destinations.
  • Filtered to your actual passport nationality — no generic info.
  • Schengen 90/180-day tracker built in.
  • Fully available offline — cached at trip setup.

Gmail Import

  • Connect Gmail and Roam Wyld finds your travel confirmation emails automatically.
  • Review and select individual bookings before adding to your trip.
  • Read-only access — we never send, delete, or modify your email.

Offline Access

  • Itinerary, entry requirements, transit directions, and emergency info all cached locally.
  • Works in airplane mode, on a cruise ship, or anywhere without signal.

Transit Directions

  • AI-generated step-by-step transit directions for every travel segment.
  • Generated once and cached offline — no internet needed mid-trip.

Companion Features

  • Emergency numbers for every country on your trip — police, ambulance, fire, embassy.
  • Currency converter with real-time exchange rates and offline cached fallback.
  • Tipping customs per country.
  • Key phrases in the local language, surfaced by your itinerary day.
  • Travel insurance reference storage.

Always getting better.
